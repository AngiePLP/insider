Seasonal Home Tip
Winter work that pays off in spring
Before the deep freeze: unhook garden hoses, cover outdoor spigots, and change your furnace filter. Thinking about selling next spring? Winter is the perfect time for the small fixes buyers notice first, like touch-up paint, caulk, and burned-out bulbs. The homeowners who sell quickly in the spring were usually preparing during the winter. Let me come by and point out what to fix now and what to leave alone. (Hint: There is a strategy behind leaving certain flaws.)
